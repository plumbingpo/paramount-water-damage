A burst pipe is one of the most common water damage events a property can face, and potentially one of the most destructive. A single burst pipe can release 4 to 8 gallons of water per minute, which is 240 to 480 gallons per hour flooding through your walls, ceilings and floors. Whether the cause is freezing temperatures, aging plumbing, excessive water pressure, corrosion or physical damage, a burst pipe demands immediate emergency response to limit the damage water does to structural materials, flooring, personal belongings and building systems.

Day or night, Paramount Restoration responds to burst pipe emergencies across Paramount and the nearby communities. Our restoration technicians can help you locate and shut off your water supply, and we coordinate with licensed plumbers so the plumbing repair is done properly. Once the water source is stopped, we immediately begin extraction with truck-mounted and portable equipment, removing standing water from all affected areas as quickly as possible.

Structural drying comes next and covers the wet drywall, the framing, the insulation, the subfloor and the finished flooring above it. Gravity carries the water through walls, floors and ceilings, so damage from one burst commonly shows up in several rooms and on different floors. Hidden moisture leads to mold and a deteriorating structure, so we use moisture detection equipment and thermal imaging to map the water's path and make sure all affected areas get dried.

When to Call for Burst Pipe Repair in Paramount

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • A fixture that stops flowing when it is below freezing outside
  • An unusually high water bill that points to a hidden leak
  • Damp, moldy odors around sinks, tubs or walls that hide pipes
  • Water pooling on floors with no apparent source
  • Tap water that turns brown, yellow or cloudy
  • A stained, soggy or bowed section of drywall overhead or beside you
  • The sound of running water when no fixtures are in use

Burst Pipe Repair Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Wrap any bare pipe that runs through a crawl space, garage or attic without heat
  • Pressure higher than 80 PSI wears on pipes and their fittings, so test yours from time to time
  • If your home still has polybutylene or aging galvanized steel lines, replace them before one gives out
  • Find the main shutoff valve now and practice closing it so you can do it fast
  • Have a licensed plumber inspect your plumbing system every few years for wear and corrosion
  • Outdoor faucets should be closed, drained and free of hoses ahead of freezing weather
